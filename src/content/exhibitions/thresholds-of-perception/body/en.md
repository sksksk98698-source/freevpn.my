## The Economics of Bandwidth: Why There Is No Free Lunch

Every packet traveling from a local device across borders must pass through multiple tier-1 telecommunication backbones and submarine cables. A 1 Gbps international private line incurs substantial monthly costs.

When a VPN provides free access, it must recover server capital, transit leasing, and operational overhead. If users do not pay with money, **their private network telemetry, local compute resources, and browsing history become the commercial inventory**.

## 3 Critical Security and Privacy Traps of Free VPNs

1. **Log Brokerage and Behavioral Monetization**: Free providers quietly harvest device identifiers, unencrypted DNS lookups, and exact URLs to sell to global data brokers.
2. **Devices Enlisted in Residential Botnets**: Security researchers have repeatedly demonstrated free VPN installers turning client machines into residential exit hops for third-party traffic.
3. **DNS Injection and MITM Attacks**: Intercepting plaintext sessions to inject aggressive ad popups or redirect financial queries.
